# Brand assets

Print and social material for Kilnin' it with Chloe. The website uses its own copies in `src/assets/` and `public/`, so changing files here doesn't change the site.

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `logo/` | The master logo (`kilninit-logo.png`, the source of the site's mark and favicons) and a square logo with the Instagram handle. |
| `stickers/` | Round logo stickers (1181 × 1181 px, 10 cm at 300 dpi): plain, on a clay texture, and on clay with the name. |
| `instagram-highlights/` | Covers for the Instagram story highlights: About me, My process, My recipes on pottery, Things that inspire me. |
| `piece-cards/` | The small card that goes with each piece sold, with an example for the page holder. |

## Piece cards

One card per piece, printed double-sided, 600 × 1050 px (2 × 3.5 in, business-card size, at 300 dpi).

- **Front:** cream background, the mauve "LJ" mark, "Kilnin' it" in Playfair Display with "with Chloe" under it, then a line with **piece name | One of a kind**, and `@kilninitwithchloe` in mauve at the bottom.
- **Back:** a photo of the piece, filling the card.

To make cards for every piece, the name comes from the inventory (`/admin` → Pieces) and the photo from the piece's own photo in the admin.
